fix: **GPU surfaces no longer speed up after input on Windows**: moving the mouse over (or restoring) an animating shared-GPU surface re-armed its frame emission while the pending winmm deadline or direct post was still on its way, so both fired and each started a self-re-arming frame chain. A few mouse moves took a 60 fps canvas to ~900 fps and its process from ~2% to ~19% CPU, permanently. Every scheduled emission now carries a process-wide generation and only the current one emits; a re-arm that cannot be scheduled keeps the old emission, and a failed direct post falls back to a timer.
