feature: **Content-scaling window resize (Windows)**: `WindowOptions.content_zoom` (also on `ShellWindow`, `UiApp` window descriptors and `RunOptions.startup_content_zoom`) makes a user resize keep the content's logical size and scale it instead: aspect locked, clamped to `min_content_zoom`..`max_content_zoom`, re-rasterized at the device scale times the zoom; `WindowState.content_zoom` reports it. Resizable transparent popups get an edge resize band on their draggable surface.
